# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective interior-design clients, studios, collaborators, recruiters, and peers evaluating Dheer Chheda's work, process, and point of view.

## Product Purpose

Present Dheer's portfolio as a concise, memorable walkthrough of his projects and design process. Success means visitors quickly understand how he thinks, trust the quality of his work, and can contact or follow him without friction.

## Brand Personality

Intriguing, accessible, thoughtful. The site should feel cinematic and personal while keeping the architecture and interiors—not the interface—as the focus.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic portfolio templates, SaaS-style card grids, decorative effects without narrative purpose, and motion that competes with the work or makes navigation feel resistant.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the work and let each project tell a clear story.
- Make feeling and intent visible before explaining form.
- Use motion only when it strengthens pacing or spatial understanding.
- Keep navigation and contact paths immediate and predictable.
- Preserve a distinctive editorial voice without sacrificing clarity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain keyboard-visible focus, semantic structure, sufficient contrast, useful alternative text, and static or reduced-motion fallbacks for motion-heavy experiences.
